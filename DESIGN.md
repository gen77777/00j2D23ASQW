# 厳 正傑 Portfolio — Design direction

## Concept

A typesetter’s proof sheet in motion. The visual idea comes from the actual career: packaging, typography, production constraints, and a transition into web development. Real career content remains the substance; moving type provides the visual identity. No template or stock imagery is used.

The original #0a0a0b background, #ffffff type, and neutral white-opacity rules. There are no chromatic accents or warm paper panels. Large Latin display type establishes rhythm; Japanese text carries the explanation and retains comfortable line heights. The typographic cylinder stays to the right on desktop and follows the introduction on mobile. The career section uses large dates and rules to mark the shift from a personal statement to evidence, while retaining the same black background.

## Research consulted

- Awwwards, Typography in Motion: https://assets.awwwards.com/assets/files/live-presentation.pdf — typography and interaction as the visual material, rather than decorative imagery.
- Dennis Snellenberg, About: https://dennissnellenberg.com/about — the designer’s stated emphasis on custom development, micro animations, transitions and interaction.
- Dennis Snellenberg, portfolio: https://dennissnellenberg.com/ — relationship between identity, work and a direct contact invitation. His layout, copy and assets are not reused.
- Codrops, On-Scroll Typography Animations: https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/01/18/on-scroll-typography-animations/ — transform origins and controlled scroll choreography.
- Codrops, typography collection: https://tympanus.net/codrops/tag/typography/ — comparison of scroll-based type and dimensional text approaches.
- GSAP SplitText documentation: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/SplitText/ — automatic re-splitting and cleanup when fonts load or widths change.

The selections above are an editorial judgement about fit, not a claim that an exhaustive search can identify an objectively best design. No award or external evaluation is implied.

## Composition

1. A brief typographic signature entrance, without a simulated loading percentage.
2. DESIGN & CODE as a display composition, paired with the actual Japanese positioning and the 36-word cylinder. Its desktop radius is restored to the original 280px, with a half-screen right column and the original ±160px vertical span; small screens adapt proportionally.
3. A scroll-inked Japanese statement.
4. A sticky method heading and four readable principles with an active section counter.
5. A monochrome work history, large dates and the actual duties from the CV.
6. Numbered capability rows with animated disclosure and a first section open by default.
7. Education and qualifications, kept readable rather than made into a slideshow.
8. A personal direction, moving practice vocabulary, and a large contact link.

## Constraints retained

- The three placeholder projects remain deleted.
- No stock photos, project thumbnails, fabricated client work, or fabricated outcome statistics.
- Personal street address, telephone number and birth date remain excluded.
- All content remains in the existing CMS data structure; ring vocabulary and speed remain editable.
- Reduced-motion alternatives, touch layouts and visible keyboard focus are required.
- Live visual acceptance requires an available browser. Static/type checks and HTTP responses do not establish animation quality or an Awwwards score.
